ANL staking protocol with a triple reward stream: ANL + XNT + CAPY
X1 Network · Version 1.2 · August 2026

1. Summary

ANL Staking Protocol is a non-custodial staking protocol for ANL tokens running on X1 Network. A participant locks ANL for a self-declared period (minimum 7 days) and receives three reward streams: a fixed APY paid in ANL from a finite, segregated pool of 200,000,000 ANL; a daily-accrued share of XNT rewards generated by a dedicated X1 validator run by the protocol team; and a CAPY bonus — a share of a segregated pool of 20,000,000 CAPY, credited at the moment a position is claimed, proportionally to the ANL reward paid.

The protocol offers two programs: Genesis — with an elevated APY depending on the entry window (up to 20% per year), and Flexible — with a fixed 8% per year regardless of entry time. The rate assigned to a position at opening is immutable for the entire life of that position.

A position is settled on the day the declared period ends: the participant receives the ANL reward and all XNT accrued at once; at that moment the CAPY bonus is calculated and reserved, claimed separately. In Flexible, early exit returns principal in full but forfeits all rewards; Genesis positions are locked until the end of the declared period.

2. Vision and context

X1 Network is a high-performance blockchain compatible with the SVM tooling ecosystem. Validators are rewarded in the native token XNT — paid after every epoch, i.e. in practice daily. ANL Staking Protocol connects ANL holders with the real work of a validator: instead of promising yield from unlimited emission, it shares the validator's real, daily revenue and pays ANL and CAPY rewards from predetermined, finite pools.

Core principle

User principal, the ANL reward pool, the XNT pool and the CAPY pool are held in four separate vaults. The program never pays principal out of a reward vault or rewards out of the principal vault — an invariant hard-coded and covered by automated tests.

3. Reward tokenomics

ANL rewards come from a segregated pool of 200,000,000 ANL (Reward Vault). Each position accrues at its fixed annual rate; a finite pool means a natural horizon — the protocol does not mint new ANL.

XNT rewards come from the revenue of the X1 validator run by the team, under the name „ANL Staking Protocol”. The validator is already live — it receives XNT after each epoch, so the protocol accrues it to positions daily.

CAPY rewards come from a segregated pool of 20,000,000 CAPY (Capy Vault). CAPY is a bonus linked to the ANL stream: the larger the share of the ANL reward pool a position claims, the proportionally larger its share of the available CAPY (section 9).

Genesis positions are irrevocable — no early-exit option. In return they receive accrued XNT every 30 days (02:00 UTC), executed permissionlessly by the protocol's automation.

6. The Flexible program

Flexible offers a fixed 8% per year regardless of window. Position mechanics identical to Genesis; Flexible positions take part in daily XNT distribution within the 35% basket.

7. Position lifecycle

  • Opening. Program, amount (min. 1 ANL), period (min. 7 days). The position receives its APY permanently.
  • Accrual. ANL reward grows continuously; XNT credited daily.
  • Claim. ANL + XNT + principal at once; at this moment the CAPY bonus is calculated and reserved (separate claim).
  • XNT payout — Genesis. Accrued XNT every 30 days; principal and ANL until period end.
  • Exit — Flexible. Principal returns in full, but rewards are forfeited; no CAPY bonus is calculated.
Exit on day 59 of 60

A Flexible position exited on day 59 returns 100% of principal, but the ANL reward and XNT are forfeited, and no CAPY bonus is calculated. Genesis positions cannot be exited.

8. XNT rewards — the daily mechanism

  • Basket split. Daily XNT pool: 65% Genesis, 35% Flexible.
  • Split within a basket. Proportional to the position's ANL stake.
  • Active position — whose period is still running. Positions past period end do not participate.
  • Empty basket. Carries over to the next day within the basket.

Daily accrual formula

XNTi = Pday × uk × stakei / Σ stakek

where uk = 0.65 (Genesis) or 0.35 (Flexible), Pday is the XNT pool collected that day.

9. CAPY rewards — the bonus mechanism

The CAPY bonus is calculated at the moment a position is claimed, based on the ANL reward paid then:

CAPY = ANL reward × available CAPY / remaining ANL

where available CAPY is the vault balance minus reservations, and remaining ANL is 200,000,000 minus ANL rewards paid so far.

  • Proportionality. Claiming 1% of the remaining ANL pool gives 1% of the available CAPY.
  • Reservation = guarantee. The calculated amount is immediately reserved and awaits claim with no deadline.
  • Separate claim. Reserved CAPY is paid out with a separate instruction (claim_capy).
  • Exit = no bonus. Without an ANL reward, no CAPY bonus is calculated.
Example

Capy Vault 20,000,000, reservations 0, 10,000,000 ANL paid out → remaining 190,000,000. A position claims 3,288 ANL → bonus ≈ 346 CAPY, immediately reserved.

11. Risk factors

  • Smart contract risk. A staged launch limits the scale of damage.
  • Validator risk. XNT size depends on validator work; not guaranteed.
  • Variable CAPY rate. Depends on vault balance and ANL rewards paid at the moment of claim.
  • Market risk. ANL, XNT, CAPY values fluctuate. Fixed APY applies to quantity, not value.
